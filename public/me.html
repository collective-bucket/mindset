About and ethics

We are building a bucket of minds together, for fun or for research.

  • Outside, everyone is only a number. No names, no photos.
  • Your alias is shared only with your school.
  • Closed texts stay with you unless you show a field to someone. Your school also sees your four closed readings.
  • Your past versions are visible only if you open them.
  • Texts that could identify someone are flagged before publishing.
  • This is not a test result and not a validated scale.
