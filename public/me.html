About and Ethics

We are building a bucket of minds together. Analysis runs in your browser; what you write is never sent to a server and stays on your device.

  • You're only a number: Outside there is no name, photo, or status.
  • Your secrets stay locked: Your alias and closed texts are shared with no one until you add someone to your school.
  • Your history is yours: No one can see past versions of your mind unless you open them.
  • Your anonymity is protected: Details that could reveal your identity are flagged by the system before publishing.
  • This is not a diagnosis: Results are not a validated clinical test; they are a sociological compass.
