Cookies

How this static site uses cookies and local storage.

Firebase Auth may set cookies or similar storage so you stay signed in. That is required for the lake features behind sign-in.

The language switch and a few UI preferences use localStorage or sessionStorage in your browser. Scoring models may cache in the browser; text is not sent to a paid AI API.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Clearing site data signs you out and resets local preferences.
