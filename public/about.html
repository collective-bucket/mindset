Mapping a mind, and the digital trail it leaves.

A trust ecosystem, not a feed

Mindset Bucket rejects the shallow like-and-follow model of ordinary social nets. It joins the anonymous unique-number spirit of the ICQ days with modern algorithmic psychology into a radical trust ecosystem.

What is a mindset number?

Every mind that enters the lake gets a unique, unchangeable number in the order it arrived. Low numbers are the first fish into the water. When you meet someone, you share your number (#42) or your link instead of a phone number or a social account. The other person skips the interview and opens your mind map.

Layers of the reading

Your narrative texts are scored into an eight-axis compass: four open on the surface, four closed at the bottom.

    Bottom waters

    Locked. You show one field at a time in the handshake; your school sees the full closed reading.

      Alias and trust

      A mindset number never shows a face or a real name. The only name anyone ever sees is the alias, and only inside a school. Once you know that name, you are no longer strangers.

      The reading schema

      Open texts are embedded in the browser with multilingual-e5-small and scored against bipolar EN/TR anchors on each axis. Scores are approximate polarities, not a validated psychometric scale.

      Four closed axes (pressure gap, facing the net, different waters, blind-spot gulf) are visible only to you and to the school you form.

      This is not a test result. It's an approximate reading.
